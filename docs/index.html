Fast, flexible AI guardrails

Guardrails at the speed of a decision.

Jev turns complex state into typed answers without generating pages of text. Juardrails combines them into auditable policy decisions for agent tools, application actions, and your own risks—using one provider call per policy evaluation.

Why this matters

One guardrail system for many decisions

A prompt-injection detector recognizes a defined set of attacks. Juardrails can also check destructive tool use, secret exposure, unsafe external actions, and rules specific to your workflow. In TypeSafe's published workflow evaluations, Jev sits on a strong accuracy, speed, and cost frontier against the LLM configurations tested.

One integration surface

A small system with a clear boundary

Juardrails answers a focused question: should this action proceed under this policy? Your application or harness enforces the returned decision. The server keeps policy versions and evaluation traces; its authorization layer checks every request.
